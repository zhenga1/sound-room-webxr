SOUND ROOM / META QUEST 3

Your controllers,
your room.

A quick reference for the immersive VR room in Quest Browser. These controls apply after you press Enter VR.

Open room with the in-VR guide →

Start here

  1. Open Sound Room in Meta Quest Browser.
  2. Choose Enter the room to start audio, then Enter VR · Quest. Accept the immersive VR prompt.
  3. Point a controller ray at a menu button and pull its trigger to press it.
  4. Choose CONTROLS in the floating menu for this reference inside VR. Use Previous / Next to browse its four pages.

Move and change your view

Head movementLook around naturally.
Left thumbstickWalk forward, backward, or sideways relative to where you look.
Right thumbstick ← / →Turn in 30-degree steps. Let the stick return to center before turning again.
Left triggerWith the menu closed, hold and aim at the floor. A blue ring shows the destination. Release to teleport.
VIEW → Closer / FurtherMove toward or away from the scene. VR zoom moves your position; it keeps the headset field of view natural.

Open or recover the menu

Right AToggle the menu. When reopening, it appears in front of you.
Click either thumbstickAnother way to toggle the menu.
MENU / SOUNDS targetThis stays near the lower left of your view. Point either ray at it and pull the trigger to reopen and recenter the Sound tab.
VIEW tabTurn left/right, move closer/further, resize the menu, or recenter it in front of you.
CloseClose the panel before drawing or gripping an object. Walking and stick turning also work with it open.

Draw with your right hand

Right BCycle DRAW → TRACE → MOVE. The current mode appears in the menu.
DRAW + right triggerHold while moving your hand to draw a colored stroke in the air. Release to finish.
TRACE + right triggerHold and aim along the floor to draw a sound path. Release to finish. Pick its sound with Color / sound.
Left XUndo the last ink stroke.
Left YChange ink color.
Color / soundCycles both the ink color and the sound used for your next floor trace.
VIEW → Clear inkClear freehand strokes. Undo can restore the last cleared batch when no ink remains.

Close the menu to draw. A sound trace needs a line at least about half a metre long. Ink, traces and furniture save in this browser on this headset.

Place objects and mix sounds

OBJECTS tabAdd a bed, books, fan, bird, or other furnishing. More objects shows the next page.
Grip buttonWith the menu closed, point at an object and hold the side grip. Aim across the floor to move it. Release to place and save.
MOVE + right triggerAn alternative way to select and drag an object.
Rotate / RemoveSelect an object first, open the menu, then choose these in OBJECTS.
SOUND tabEach placed sound and floor trace has Mute / Play and − / + volume buttons. More sounds changes page.
Studio / CathedralIn OBJECTS, change the room's reverb while the same performance continues.

If something feels stuck

Try A, a thumbstick click, or the persistent MENU / SOUNDS target. In VIEW, choose Recenter or resize the panel. Close the menu before drawing or moving furniture.

If you still see old controls, exit VR, reload the page in Quest Browser, then enter VR again. Use the Quest system controls to exit immersive mode if necessary.

This is a fully virtual room with tracked controllers, not hand tracking or passthrough. Use your normal Quest boundary. Controls have automated checks, but physical headset behavior still needs on-device verification.
