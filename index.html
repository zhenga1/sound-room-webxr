YOUR QUICK TOUR

A room you can remix.

Walk: W/A/S/D or arrow keys. Click empty floor for mouse-look; Escape releases the cursor. Hold right mouse and drag to look without locking.

Furnish: Choose Add an object for beds, books, lamps, a bird, and more. Drag, rotate, adjust volume, or remove each one. You can add multiple copies of each object. Your layout is saved in this browser.

Draw sound: In Make it your mix, choose a sound, press Draw sound trace, and drag a line on the floor plan. The line emits sound from the point nearest you. Each sound has a different animated line effect. Change a finished trace's sound with its dropdown, or mute, adjust, redraw, and remove it.

Quest 3: Open this HTTPS page in Quest Browser and choose Enter VR. Left stick walks; right stick snap turns. Right trigger draws. Grip moves an object. A opens the library; B cycles Draw / Trace / Move. Left trigger teleports. X undoes ink. Y changes color.

Compare: Studio and Cathedral change browser reverb while keeping the performance playing.

This public WebXR edition uses original procedural audio loops, browser HRTF positioning, and synthetic reverb. It does not use SATIE recordings or the Attractor model.
